==About Money Heist== :question:
  - It is Spanish heist crime drama series created by Álex Pina that originally aired on a local network (Antena 3).
  - The show is centered around two massive and highly planned heists led by a mysterious figure known only as "The Professor".
  - Each core member of the heist team adopts a city name pseudonym (like Tokyo, Berlin, and Nairobi) to protect their identities.
  - The series became a cultural phenomenon recognized for its striking visual symbols of resistance: red jumpsuits and Salvador Dalí masks.
  - Money Heist concluded its five-part run in December 2021 and has since spawned successful spin-offs and a Korean adaptation.
___________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

==Synopsis== :movie_camera:
The show centers on two massive and lengthy attacks that target the **Royal Mint** and the **Bank of Spain**, two representations of the Spanish state's financial system.  In order to keep discipline and hide their identities, *the Professor* assembles a varied group of criminals who go by city code names. The team inside deals with hostages, internal strife, and elite law enforcement forces, while the Professor oversees operations from the outside by negotiating with authorities and controlling the police. As the motivations and backstories of the characters are revealed, the distinction between good and bad becomes more hazy.
